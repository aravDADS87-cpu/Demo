# SuperStore Sales Analysis Using Microsoft Excel

## Project Overview
- This project focuses on analyzing SuperStore sales data using Microsoft Excel to generate meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.
- The objective is to transform raw sales data into valuable information by identifying sales trends, analyzing business performance, and understanding customer purchasing behavior.
- The project demonstrates practical skills in Excel data cleaning, data analysis, advanced formulas, reporting, and data visualization.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing products and product categories.
- Understand regional and segment-wise sales performance.
- Analyze customer purchasing patterns.
- Identify monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Evaluate profitability and business growth opportunities.
- Present important business insights through an organized Excel dashboard.

## Key Features
- **Data Cleaning:** Organizing raw data, identifying missing values, removing duplicates, and preparing data for analysis.
- **Sales Analysis:** Evaluating total sales, profit, quantity, and overall business performance.
- **Product Analysis:** Identifying top-selling products and analyzing category-wise and sub-category-wise performance.
- **Regional Analysis:** Comparing sales and profitability across different regions and locations.
- **Customer Analysis:** Understanding customer purchasing behavior and identifying valuable customers.
- **Time-Based Analysis:** Analyzing monthly and yearly sales trends to understand business performance over time.
- **Data Visualization:** Creating charts, graphs, and interactive reports to communicate insights effectively.
- **Dashboard Development:** Building an Excel dashboard to present important business metrics in a simple and understandable format.

## Excel Skills & Functions Used
- XLOOKUP and Nested XLOOKUP for retrieving multiple details.
- IF and IFERROR for logical calculations and error handling.
- SUMIF and SUMIFS for conditional calculations.
- COUNTIF and COUNTIFS for counting records based on conditions.
- Pivot Tables for summarizing and analyzing large datasets.
- Pivot Charts for visual representation of sales performance.
- Conditional Formatting for highlighting important data.
- Data Validation for creating interactive selections.
- Excel Charts and Graphs for business reporting.
